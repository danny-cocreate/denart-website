## Nobody tells you about this part

Everyone prepares you for the grief. The anger. The practical chaos of a life coming apart.

Nobody mentions the identity crisis. The way you reach for habits that belong to someone you're no longer sure you are. The way familiar things feel like they're happening to a stranger. The way your own body starts to feel like a place you've been subtly evicted from — a space that still carries the old story in its posture, its tension, its reflexes.

Talk therapy helps. For a while. But there's a point where the words run out. Where you've processed the story so many times you can recite it fluently — and still feel stuck in it somewhere below the neck.

That's the place this work is for.

## Most healing asks you to talk about the transformation

**Redrawn asks your body to *be* it.**

Through a combination of guided visualization, somatic practice, and body painting, you'll access the part of you that knows who you're becoming — not intellectually, but as a felt, physical reality.

You'll leave with something no coaching session or therapy hour can give you: a tangible image of the new self, written on your own skin, witnessed by people who understand exactly where you've been.

This isn't art class. It isn't therapy. It isn't a support group.

It's a ritual.

For people who are done processing and ready to step forward.

## What Actually Happens (3 phases)

### 1. First: You go inward

Guided hypnotic visualization, your place, safe/entirely yours. Something emerges: an image, a color, a feeling that belongs to the version of you that's coming. That becomes what we paint.

### 2. Then: You release what isn't yours anymore

Using body-safe paint, you'll write directly on your skin the words living there without permission. The things that were said. The story you've been carrying. You sit with it. Then wipe it away. Then write something new. What you'd say to yourself as a child going through this. What the rebuilt version of you says back.

### 3. Finally: You're witnessed

Through simple prompts, you and the others paint each other's answers. Your superpower. What you're finally allowed to be. What this chapter revealed.

By the end, you've worn who you're becoming. You've been seen in them. Celebrated in them.

## Who This Is For

- You're somewhere on the other side of something — divorce, major loss, a life that no longer fits.
- You're past the acute phase but not yet fully in the next thing.
- You can feel it forming but can't quite inhabit it yet.
- You're open to something that doesn't look like anything you've tried before.

## Who This Isn't For

- **Not right if you're in acute crisis** — a licensed therapist is the right first step.
- **Also not right if partial skin exposure (arms, shoulders, hands) feels like too much right now.**

## About Danny

Danny Setiawan has built DenArt NYC into one of the city's most recognized body painting studios — featured on the Today Show, CBS News, and international media.

After his own divorce, he moved two blocks from his family home so his daughter could walk over anytime. One Saturday, sitting on the floor of an empty apartment assembling a bed frame that should have felt hopeful and didn't, he felt the ground go out from under him.

The thing that brought him back was an afternoon painting a canvas with his daughter — her handprints, her puppy's paw print, everything they'd built that year colored in by her small hands. A Father's Day gift. The first time in months he felt like himself.

That afternoon is why Redrawn exists.

Danny is a certified hypnotherapist (Sussex Hypnotherapy Centre) and NLP practitioner.
