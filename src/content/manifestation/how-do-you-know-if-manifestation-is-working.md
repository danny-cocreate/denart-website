If you're refreshing your phone for a text, counting 11:11s, and reading meaning into every coincidence, you already know the honest answer: you're checking because you don't yet feel like you have it. That checking is the problem, and it's also the clue.

## Stop reading tea leaves

Signs keep you looking *outside* for proof that the thing is coming. But scanning the world for evidence is an act of lack, you only look because you feel it's still missing. Every "sign" you hunt for quietly reaffirms *not yet*. The people for whom manifestation works reliably aren't watching for signs; they've stopped needing them.

## The real signal is that it feels normal

Here's the shift to watch for: the wish-fulfilled state stops feeling exciting or far off and starts feeling *ordinary*, boring, even. That's not the manifestation failing; that's Neville Goddard's "living in the end" becoming your baseline. When having the thing feels unremarkable, your subconscious has accepted it as real, and that's the state results follow from.

## Watch your behavior, not the sky

The most trustworthy evidence is that you start thinking and acting like the person who already has it, making different decisions, holding yourself differently, finishing what you'd stalled on. One artist I worked with knew it had taken not because of a sign, but because she was suddenly *making art again*, finishing pieces, framing them, showing them off. The state had become her, so the behavior changed.

## Why "signs that don't come true" happen

If you've had striking signs and then nothing, you probably reached the state for a moment, enough to register a flicker in the outside world, but couldn't hold it. It snapped back to your old baseline before it became normal. Signs mark a momentary shift; results follow a sustained one. That gap is exactly why holding the state day to day matters more than reaching it once, and why a physical anchor you can return to (see [why you can't hold the wish-fulfilled state on your own](/manifestation/why-you-cant-hold-the-wish-fulfilled-state)) does more than any sign.
